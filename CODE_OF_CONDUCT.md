# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Mem0Sharp community a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Reporting problems privately when public discussion could expose someone to harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized language or
attention, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct
that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of
conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that violate it, and may take other actions they
consider appropriate.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through a private
GitHub channel. For security vulnerabilities, follow the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

All reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against
anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is
representing the project or its community.
